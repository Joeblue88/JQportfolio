BIO /

I’m Joe (Liyuan) Qiao — a product designer with over ten years of commercial experience across operating systems, IoT, big-data & AI, and B2C/B2B digital services.

I shape ideas into shipped products through design thinking, user research, and a pixel-perfect eye for details. Have dived into harness design&engineering since 2022.

Since 2022, I have heavily focused on harnessing AI and engineering implementation—utilizing AI-driven workflows to orchestrate, accelerate, and bridge the gap between design mastery and technical execution.

Today I lead design system work at Verisure, covering design language&guidelines,, component libraries, frontend deliverables.

Before that I spent years inside Huawei’s product design teams, working on EMUI, the next-generation OS, and enterprise communication tools used by millions every day.
